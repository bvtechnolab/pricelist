Sales Multi Pricelist per Order Line

Maximize sales flexibility. Apply different pricelists on each sale order line with an advanced comparison wizard.

🚀 Overview & Business Benefits

Sales Multi Pricelist per Order Line is the ultimate pricing tool for your Odoo Sales application. It breaks the standard limitation of a single pricelist per order, empowering your sales team to assign different pricelists to individual order lines independently.

Whether you are mixing wholesale and retail items, applying special promotional pricelists to specific products, or negotiating line-by-line with a customer, this module gives you the granular control you need to close deals faster and more profitably.

⭐ Why Choose This Module?

  • Increase Profit Margins: Stop giving blanket discounts across an entire order. Tailor your pricing per item to protect your bottom line.
  • Data-Driven Decisions: Instantly see the Cost, Margin amount, and Margin % for every pricelist before applying it.
  • Save Time: Use the built-in intuitive wizard to compare pricelists directly from the quotation line.
  • Seamless Integration: Works flawlessly with Odoo's native taxes, multi-currency, and UoM conversions.

💻 Key Features

✅ Per-Line Pricelist

Assign a completely different pricelist to each sale order line independently.

📊 Comparison Wizard

View all pricelists side-by-side in a smart table with Unit Price, Cost, and Margin.

💰 Margin Visibility

Empower your sales reps by displaying margin percentages clearly before they quote.

⚙ Toggle Configuration

Easily enable or disable the feature globally from the Sales Configuration Settings.

💲 Multi-Currency Support

Prices are flawlessly computed using proper currency exchange rates from the order.

💪 Taxes Auto-Recompute

Taxes and totals are automatically and instantly recalculated after applying a new pricelist.

📝 How It Works

Step 1: Enable the Feature in Settings

Navigate to Sales → Configuration → Settings. Under the Pricing section, enable the "Multi Pricelist per Line" option and save.

Step 2: Click "Apply" on any Order Line

On your Sale Order, a new "Line Pricelist" column and an "Apply" button will appear on each line. Click the button to launch the comparison tool.

Step 3: Compare and Apply

The comparison wizard clearly displays all active pricelists along with the calculated Unit Price, Unit Cost, Margin, and Margin (%). Simply click "Apply" on the best option, and the line is updated instantly.
